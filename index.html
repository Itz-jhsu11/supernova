SAT Nova ✦ Live

A gamified, Duolingo-style Digital SAT prep app. A personalized day-by-day roadmap that ends on your test date, learn-first lessons with worked math, a near-infinite question engine, adaptive Bluebook-style full exams scaled to 1600, and Nova — a Socratic AI coach that guides with questions instead of just giving answers.

How it helps: turns months of intimidating SAT prep into a clear daily rhythm — three lessons and a timed test a day — so students walk into test day already knowing the path they took to get there.

NOVA Day ✦ Live

A student schedule-optimizer for after school. Brain-dump your tasks — category, importance, due date, estimated time — and NOVA Day builds an optimized, time-blocked evening that fits before your bedtime, with automatic breaks, a live run timer, 5-minutes-left reminders, and a floating picture-in-picture timer.

How it helps: replaces the nightly "where do I even start?" spiral with a plan that respects sleep — the schedule always ends before bedtime, and anything that won't fit is flagged honestly as overflow.

Open NOVA Day →

NOVA Market ✦ Live

Track the market, find out what actually moved it, and learn the economics underneath — explained at the depth of the classes you've taken. Daily market narratives in plain sentences, deep dives on single stocks, a screener where every filter comes with its own counter-argument, and ten lessons anchored to live prices.

How it helps: builds real financial literacy before adulthood — students learn to separate signal from noise and to hear the counter-argument to every investing idea, using live markets as the textbook.

Open NOVA Market →

Nova Portfolio ✦ Live

A permanent, private-by-default record of everything a student has actually done — activities, projects, awards, and work — built up as it happens instead of reconstructed in a panic senior year.

How it helps: when applications, résumés, and scholarship forms arrive, the work is already written down. Nothing gets forgotten, and nothing is shared unless the student explicitly turns it on.

Open Nova Portfolio →

Nova Research ✦ Live

A research project planner that gets a student from "I'm interested in something" to "I did real work on it." AI-shaped project plans, candidate research questions, and a structure built around one enemy: the long quiet middle where projects usually die.

How it helps: every design decision is judged by one question — does this make the project easier to return to after a gap? Students can drift for weeks and still pick the thread back up.

Open Nova Research →

Unwavering Nova ✦ Coming soon

A habit and consistency tracker built for real student life. Streaks that survive dropped connections and double taps, catch-up logging for the days you forgot, gentle opt-in reminders, and unlimited habits — free, forever.

How it helps: reports a 70% completion rate as 70%, not as a broken streak. No punishment, no leaderboards, no body-tracking — just honest consistency, which is what actually builds habits.

Launching soon ✧

Nova ID ✦ Live

The identity layer under everything. One student = one Nova account: sign-in, email verification, sessions, and a small shared profile. Every app in the suite signs in through here and keeps its own data in its own store.

How it helps: one username, one password, zero re-registration — and because each app stores only its own data, a student's information never pools in one place.
