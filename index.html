✦ Why these were made

Every app in SUPERNOVA started as a real problem in a real student's week — and a belief that the tools students are given shouldn't work against them.

Test prep felt like a mazeSAT Nova was made so prep is a clear daily path to a date on the calendar — not an intimidating pile of practice tests.
Evenings kept vanishingNOVA Day was made because "I'll figure out my homework order later" costs an hour every night — and usually costs sleep too.
Projects died in the quiet middleNova Research was made for the long gap between "this is interesting" and "I actually did it," where good ideas usually go silent.
Achievements got forgottenNova Portfolio was made so that senior-year applications draw from a record built all along, not from a panicked memory dump.
Habit apps punished peopleUnwavering Nova was made in protest of broken-streak guilt — consistency should be celebrated honestly, never weaponized.
Money felt like a foreign languageNOVA Market was made so students learn economics from live markets, with every claim paired with its counter-argument.
